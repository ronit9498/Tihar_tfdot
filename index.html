Five days, five stories

  1. Day 1

    Kag Tihar काग तिहार

    Crows are the messengers of the festival. Families leave food on the roof and listen for their calls at dawn.

  2. Day 2

    Kukur Tihar कुकुर तिहार

    Dogs are thanked for their loyalty. They get a marigold garland, a red tika on the forehead, and a special meal.

  3. Day 3

    Laxmi Puja लक्ष्मी पूजा

    Cows are honoured in the morning. At night, every home glows with diyos and rangoli to welcome Goddess Laxmi. Children sing Deusi-Bhailo from door to door.

  4. Day 4

    Govardhan Puja गोवर्धन पूजा

    Oxen are worshipped for their hard work in the fields. In the Newar community, this is also Mha Puja, a day to honour your own self.

  5. Day 5

    Bhai Tika भाइ टीका

    Sisters put a seven-colour tika on their brothers' foreheads and pray for their long life. Brothers give gifts in return.

Why light a diyo?

In Tihar, light pushes the dark away. A small flame in every window tells the whole street that someone is welcome here.
